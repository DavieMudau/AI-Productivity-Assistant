# AI-Productivity-Assistant
AI Workplace Productivity Assistant
AI Workmate
AI Workmate is an AI-powered workplace productivity dashboard that helps you automate the busywork and stay focused on meaningful work. Choose a tool, add a little context, and your AI assistant takes it from there.

Live site: https://ai-workplace-product-saxu.bolt.host/

Overview
AI Workmate brings a suite of focused AI tools into one clean, calm workspace. Instead of juggling separate apps for writing, note-taking, planning, and research, you pick the tool that fits the task, describe what you're working on, and get a useful result in seconds — all tracked in a personal productivity overview.

Key Features
🧰 Your Toolkit
Tool	What it does
Smart Email Generator	Compose thoughtful emails in seconds, tuned to your tone (Professional, Friendly, Concise, Persuasive) and audience (Clients, Team members, Leadership, Partners)
Meeting Notes	Turn conversations into clear, actionable takeaways
AI Task Planner	Prioritize your day with confidence
Research Assistant	Find signal in a sea of information
Every tool uses structured prompts tuned for clear, useful outputs.

🤖 AI Assistant Panel
Tell the assistant what you're working on
Select a tone and audience to shape the output
Add optional context for more tailored results
Hit Generate with AI and see your result appear in the output panel
📊 Your Momentum
A productivity overview that keeps you motivated:

A live task completion percentage (e.g. 72% — "You're in a good flow")
Daily progress such as "8 of 12 daily tasks completed"
A count of tasks remaining today
Recent activity — a feed of what you've accomplished and when (e.g. "Client proposal follow-up drafted · Smart Email Generator · 18m ago")
➕ New Workspace Task
Quickly capture new tasks from the dashboard so nothing slips through the cracks.

How to Use
Open the dashboard and pick a tool from "Your toolkit."
Describe your task — what you're working on, plus optional context.
Choose tone and audience where applicable (e.g. email tone: Professional, Friendly, Concise, Persuasive).
Click Generate with AI and review the result.
Track your momentum in the productivity overview and recent activity feed.
Design Philosophy
Automate the busywork. Let AI handle first drafts, summaries, and planning so you can focus on what matters.
Make space for great work. A minimal, focused interface designed around one question: What are you working on?
Human in the loop. AI-generated content may require human review — you always review before you use.
Notes
The dashboard greets you personally (e.g. "Good morning, Alex ✦") with the current date.
Recent activity and momentum stats update as you use the tools throughout the day.
